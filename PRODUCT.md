# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Partners who want to understand, remember, and lovingly support their partner's menstrual cycle with empathy, sensitivity, and zero awkwardness; plus couples sharing the device.

## Product Purpose
Provide an intimate, privacy-first, offline-ready companion that translates cycle tracking into daily caring actions, timely heads-up reminders, mood tracking, and shared warmth rather than cold clinical metrics.

## Positioning
HerCycle is crafted specifically for the emotional reality of loving relationships: not an impersonal sterile calendar, but an intimate, considerate companion centered on "Take care of her today" — empathy, thoughtful preparation, warmth, and genuine partner support.

## Operating Context
Personal mobile and desktop web accessed during everyday intimate moments: checking in the morning, picking up groceries or comfort treats, logging moods together in the evening, or setting gentle discreet notifications. Stored locally on-device with optional PIN protection and Firebase sync.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Offline-first storage with local storage state and optional Firebase synchronization.
- PIN lock security and multiple profile management.
- Cycle forecasting: next period countdown, ovulation day, fertile window, PMS anticipation, and 12-month projections.
- Daily caring tips, love reminders, and curated inspirational quotes.
- Daily mood, physical symptom, and menstrual flow tracking.
- Health insights by phase, personal love notes, and couple memory log.
- Constraint: Predictions are mathematical estimates based on cycle averages, not medical diagnoses.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: HerCycle ❤️
- Voice: Tender, attentive, reassuring, mature, considerate, and deeply respectful.
- Visual World: Warm Botanical & Intimate Editorial — dusky rose, terracotta, warm cream/linen surfaces, deep fig/velvet plum dark mode, refined typography (Fraunces + Quicksand), organic fluid motifs, and tactile apothecary warmth.

## Evidence on Hand
- Algorithmic cycle calculations in `src/lib/cycle.ts`.
- Curated love tips and quotes in `src/lib/content.ts`.
- Reactive local store and data sync in `src/lib/store.ts` and `src/lib/store-context.tsx`.

## Product Principles
1. **Empathy over clinical jargon**: Speak the language of care, reassurance, and intimacy.
2. **Privacy is sacred**: Data stays strictly on-device with instant PIN protection.
3. **Actionable affection**: Don't just show cycle numbers; provide the exact caring gesture or comfort that helps right now.
4. **Keepsake craft**: Design every surface to feel like an heirloom personal journal or cherished love note.
